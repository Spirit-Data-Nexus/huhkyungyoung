

## 240531_The_Truth_of_Vibration


## Date: 2014-05-31


## Keywords: Vibration, Sound, Creation, Humanity, Consciousness


## Content


## The Primacy of Sound and Vibration 
Huh Kyung young posits that the universe operates in a manner contrary to human perception, emphasizing the fundamental role of sound and vibration in creation. He argues that sound precedes and creates humanity, rather than being merely a product of human vocalization. This concept extends to the very fabric of existence, where sound, understood as vibration or wavelength, is the initial force from which light, spirit, mind, and consciousness emerge, ultimately manifesting even in physical attributes like hair. This perspective suggests that human beings often perceive the world in reverse, failing to recognize the foundational creative power of sound.


## The Paradox of Parenthood and Filial Piety 
Huh Kyung young presents a paradoxical view of parenthood, asserting that children "give birth" to their parents. He explains that a couple only becomes "parents" when they have a child, as the child bestows the title and identity of "parent" upon them. Without children, a couple, regardless of how long they have been together, cannot truly be considered parents. This implies that children hold a position of significant importance, even a "master" status, because they are the ones who elevate individuals to the esteemed role of parenthood. The profound responsibility of parents to protect their children stems from this dynamic, as the loss of a child means the loss of their parental identity.


## The Significance of DNA and Lineage 
DNA is presented as the true connector between parents and children, rather than blood. Huh Kyung young explains that while blood types can differ between mother and child, DNA unequivocally links individuals to their lineage, revealing their precise family origins. He illustrates this by noting that a single strand of hair contains enough DNA to potentially create millions of individuals, highlighting its immense informational capacity. The combination of parental DNA creates a new, unique DNA in the child, signifying a distinct individual rather than a mere extension of the parents.


## The Divine Blueprint of Human Conception 
The process of human conception and development is described as a divinely orchestrated miracle. Huh Kyung young details how male and female DNA combine, with each contributing 23 chromosomes to form the 46 chromosomes of a new individual. He emphasizes that the mother's blood does not directly enter the fetus; instead, the fetus independently generates its own blood and organs, with the mother's body serving to filter waste products through the amniotic membrane. This intricate process, he asserts, is a testament to the Creator's design, a secret of genetics established billions of years ago. He claims to be the one who understands and embodies this creative involvement in the human form.


## The Prophetic Power of Sound and National Destiny 
Huh Kyung young asserts that sound holds prophetic power, allowing him to foresee future events and the destinies of nations. He cites instances where his pronouncements regarding political events, such as former President Park Geun-hye's "unification is a jackpot" statement leading to national turmoil, or Samsung's "Mach management" preceding the late Lee Kun-hee's heart attack, proved accurate. He attributes this ability to his deep understanding of the "secret of sound," which he claims is the highest principle in the universe, even above appearance and mind. This understanding allows him to instantly grasp the implications of words and names, predicting outcomes with remarkable speed and accuracy. He also connects this to the Korean people's unique linguistic ability, particularly their "ipsong-eum" (vocal sound), which he believes signifies their destiny to unify the world and their superior intellect.


## The Unity of Buddha and Christ: A Reinterpretation of Divinity 
Huh Kyung young argues that the teachings of Buddha and Jesus are fundamentally identical, despite conventional religious interpretations. He states that Jesus's message of equality, where helping the marginalized is equivalent to helping God, aligns with Buddha's teaching that every human being is a Buddha. Both figures, he contends, emphasize the inherent divinity and worth of every individual, regardless of their social status or actions. He criticizes traditional Christianity for rejecting "pluralism," which he sees as a misunderstanding of this universal divinity. He suggests that recognizing the divine in all things and all people, from a simple cloth to a piece of paper, fosters compassion and prevents the commission of sin.


## The True Meaning of Happiness: Beyond Possession to Contribution 
Huh Kyung young redefines happiness, distinguishing between "passive happiness" (쾌락, pleasure/possession) and "active happiness" (희락, joy/contribution). He warns that the pursuit of passive happiness, driven by material possession and self-gratification, inevitably leads to global catastrophe and unhappiness. True happiness, or "joy," arises from giving to others and contributing to the greater good. He uses the analogy of bees, whose purpose is not to consume honey for their own pleasure but to pollinate flowers, thereby ensuring the survival of nature. Similarly, humans are not meant to accumulate wealth for personal satisfaction but to fulfill a divine mission of creation and contribution. He emphasizes that "ignorance" (무지) is the root of evil, as a lack of wisdom leads to actions that harm oneself and others, even if unintentionally.


## The Infinite Universe and the Creator's Design 
The universe is described as infinite in both space and time, a concept that transcends human comprehension and conventional religious narratives. Huh Kyung young states that the universe, with its countless stars and boundless space, has existed from eternity, not created at a specific point. While local galaxies and solar systems may be created and destroyed in cycles, the universe itself is eternal. He illustrates the Creator's power by describing how a single asteroid the size of Jeju Island could obliterate Earth in 0.1 seconds, turning it into dust. This power, he claims, is within his grasp, demonstrating his control over Earth's destiny. He asserts that this profound understanding of the universe's infinite nature and the Creator's power is what allows him to heal and transform lives through the mere mention of his name.


## The Korean People as a Celestial Race 
Huh Kyung young proclaims the Korean people as a "celestial race" (천손 민족) with a unique destiny to lead the world. He highlights their linguistic superiority, particularly their ability to pronounce certain sounds that other nations cannot, which he links to their advanced intellect and their role as the "Messiah" nation. He asserts that Korea's historical influence extended across Asia and even to the Americas, citing the Apache people as descendants of Koreans and claiming that the names of places like the Rocky Mountains and the Himalayas have Korean origins. He also points to the fact that the Jewish people, despite their advanced linguistic expression, lack their own written language, unlike Koreans. He suggests that the Korean people's historical dominance was lost but is now being restored through his presence, as he has come to this land to fulfill its destiny of global unification.
