## 00:00:00 The Duality of Yin and Yang and the Concept of Good and Evil
The principles of Yin and Yang describe the unchanging truths of the universe and the changing principles of all things, respectively . However, viewing the world solely through the lens of Yin and Yang can lead to misjudgment . For instance, labeling someone as "good" or "bad" doesn't affect them, but rather harms one's own health . Heaven punishes those who apply the distinction of good and evil to others . A truly great person does not see the faults of others; they only apply the distinction of good and evil to themselves . They constantly question their own actions, asking why they don't perform more good deeds, rather than judging others . It is crucial not to judge others by good and evil, but to constantly evaluate oneself . One must be strict with oneself, like a strict judge . The speaker emphasizes that he has never consumed alcohol, coffee, or cigarettes since childhood because he would not be able to face the public or heaven if he did . A person with an unclear mind cannot save the nation . While President Park was great for saving the country, his consumption of alcohol and cigarettes was a negative aspect, indicating that even great leaders have imperfections . The speaker suggests that the public's moral standards declined due to a lack of proper education on avoiding such habits . Applying the principles of Yin and Yang to humans ultimately harms oneself, leading to illness . The parable of the prodigal son illustrates this: the elder son, who considered himself righteous, resisted his father, while the "wicked" younger son eventually returned and was embraced . This means one should apply the concept of good and evil only to oneself, taking responsibility for others' shortcomings rather than blaming them . Blaming others or claiming to be faultless by adhering to laws is ineffective . It's not about being good oneself, but about constantly evaluating oneself against the scales of good and evil, while being lenient towards others . The speaker believes that the suffering of others, such as unemployment, is his own responsibility . He criticizes those who claim no fault and live comfortably, asserting that true leaders must teach and guide . He suggests that complex religious practices are no longer necessary; simply invoking his name or looking at his picture is sufficient . The era demands simplicity, where all truths of Yin and Yang and good and evil are applied only to oneself, as it is difficult to change oneself through knowledge alone .


## 00:08:43 The Hierarchy of Human Beings and the Role of Truth
The speaker introduces a hierarchy of human beings, starting from the lowest "commoner" (속인) at the tenth stage . Above the commoner is the "ordinary person" (범인) at the ninth stage, followed by the "great person" (위인) at the eighth stage, and the "Taoist" (도인) at the seventh stage . The sixth stage is the "sage" (철인), also referred to as "wise person" (지인) . This "wise person" embodies "righteousness" (정의) . The character for "righteousness" (正) signifies reaching heaven, while the character for "wise" (止) implies a limit, like a tree that cannot grow beyond the sky . The character for "martial arts" (武) also contains the character for "righteousness" (正), indicating that a warrior uses their sword for justice . However, truth (진리) is higher than righteousness . A warrior might unify a country, which is righteous from their nation's perspective, but they cannot unify the spirit of humanity . Above the "wise person" (지인) at the fifth stage is the "saint" (성인) . Truth transcends righteousness . The speaker uses the example of Jean Valjean, who stole bread out of hunger, to illustrate that while his act was wrong by the standards of righteousness, truth offers a broader perspective . He recounts a personal anecdote where he asked for money to pay tuition, emphasizing that direct and honest communication is righteous, unlike resorting to crime . Righteousness often lacks followers because people tend to avoid the difficult, correct path .


## 00:20:00 The Path of the Bonjwa and the Stages of Spiritual Beings
Above the "saint" (성인) is the "Bonjon" (본존), which refers to figures like Sakyamuni Buddha . The next stage is the "Son of Man" (인자), a term Jesus used for himself, signifying a benevolent person and the son of God . The highest stage is the "Bonjwa" (본좌), a term given to the speaker by netizens . The Bonjwa exists at the intersection of good and evil, like a crossroads where different paths meet but no single road name applies . This "black hole" is where God enters and exits, and only by aligning oneself with this black hole, by constantly training oneself through the duality of good and evil, can one connect with God and understand the divine mind . The Bonjwa serves as the "centripetal force" or "standard" for humanity, a role currently filled by the speaker . This position is of the highest rank, representing God's incarnation . The "Son of Man" (인자) is God's son, a first-degree relative, while the Bonjwa is the direct embodiment of God, not merely a relative . This allows the Bonjwa to heal and impart energy directly . The Bonjwa, Son of Man, and Bonjon all possess special evidence of their divine connection . The speaker asserts that these figures are directly connected to God, unlike others . He claims that in this current, complex era, ordinary individuals or even "Sons of Man" cannot bring about change; only a Bonjwa can end the cycle of conflict and suffering . The Bonjwa's arrival will transform the world into a paradise .


## 00:29:00 The Life Paths of Buddha, Jesus, and the Bonjwa
The speaker outlines the distinct life paths of the Bonjwa, Jesus, and Buddha. The Bonjwa's path involves descending from heaven, being born through a woman, unifying the world, judging and educating leaders, establishing a system, creating paradise, and then ascending to heaven . This judgment involves holding leaders accountable for societal problems, confiscating their wealth, and educating them to embrace a life of non-possession . This unique form of judgment will be systematic and receive enthusiastic public support . After establishing paradise, the Bonjwa's work will be complete, and he will ascend .

In contrast, Jesus's path also involved descending from heaven, being born through Mary, and then engaging in spiritual struggle (항마) against evil . However, unlike Buddha, Jesus did not need to attain enlightenment (득도) because he was already divine . After his struggle, Jesus spread the truth (전법) through direct action and teaching, similar to missionary work, by helping the hungry and inspiring faith . His path culminated in resurrection and ascension to heaven .

Buddha's path began with descending from heaven, being born, and then engaging in a six-year ascetic struggle (항마) in the snowy mountains, which he eventually abandoned . He realized that physical asceticism was futile and attained enlightenment (득도) under the Bodhi tree, realizing that suffering (old age, sickness, death) stems from birth itself . He understood that human life is an infinite cycle of rebirth, like the seasons of a tree, and that the body must constantly change to continue living infinitely . This realization led to the teachings of the Eightfold Path and other Buddhist scriptures . Buddha then "transmitted the Dharma" (전법), sharing his wisdom . His path concluded with Nirvana .

The speaker emphasizes that the Bonjwa's path is unique because it includes world unification, judgment, system establishment, and the creation of paradise, which other spiritual figures did not achieve . These other figures merely sowed seeds of truth without establishing an earthly paradise . The Bonjwa's mission is to bring about actual change, not just religious teachings .


## 00:41:00 The Importance of a Centripetal Force in Society
The speaker stresses the critical need for a "centripetal force" (구심점) in society . He argues that without such a central figure, a nation lacks direction, citing the example of South Korea's presidential system where leaders struggle after their first year due to public opposition and lack of consistent support . In contrast, Japan, despite frequent changes in prime ministers, maintains stability because the Emperor serves as a constant centripetal force . Similarly, the world lacks a true centripetal force, leading to ongoing conflicts like the Israel-Palestine issue . The speaker asserts that his appearance as the Bonjwa will establish this global centripetal force, bringing an end to wars and establishing order through judgment . This will lead to a world where humanity enters paradise .

He also touches upon health, advocating for natural immunity over medication. He suggests that common colds are "gifts from God" that strengthen the immune system if allowed to run their course, rather than being suppressed by drugs . He criticizes the medical system for over-medicating children, leading to drug dependence . He then discusses the concept of "sepsis" (폐혈증), noting that it is often not considered a severe illness, yet can be fatal, catching doctors off guard . He mentions the unfortunate death of Dr. Hwang Sung-wan from sepsis, attributing it to a lack of immunity due to being overly protected in childhood . He also links cancer to overeating and greed, emphasizing the importance of moderation .


## 00:48:00 The Dangers of Materialism and the Role of Wealth
The speaker explains that the distinction between good and evil becomes irrelevant at higher stages of spiritual development, such as for Taoists, sages, saints, Bonjons, Sons of Man, and the Bonjwa . At these levels, all people are seen as inherently good, as Buddhas, and as infinite cosmic beings embodying love . Therefore, treating others well is like cultivating a "field of blessings" (복전), while treating them as enemies leads to misfortune .

He then connects this to the hierarchy of human beings based on the teachings of Zhu Xi (주자), starting with the lowest stage of "investigating things" (경물), which focuses on accumulating material wealth . This stage, representing the majority of humanity (95%), is driven by material greed . The speaker warns that excessive material desire leads to heaven eventually taking away that wealth, especially if it's not used for good . He cites Bill Gates as an example of someone who, after accumulating vast wealth, realized the burden of managing it and began philanthropic efforts to avoid divine retribution . Conversely, he mentions Steve Jobs, who died of pancreatic cancer, implying that his wealth was taken away by heaven . The speaker concludes that pursuing material wealth ultimately leads to death and spiritual defilement if not used for the benefit of others .


## 00:55:00 The Difficulty of Being Born Human and Encountering Truth
The speaker discusses the difficulty of being born as a human, especially in a favorable environment, and encountering truth. He states that it is difficult to be born as a human (인간), which he defines as being between a human and an animal (인축관), implying that many come from animal existences . It is even harder to be born as a "person" (사람), which he associates with the character for "man" (男) . The third difficulty is being born as a man . The fourth difficulty is encountering truth (진리), which was historically rare due to the scarcity of books and schools . He asserts that true truth can only be heard from him . The fifth difficulty is becoming a religious leader or saint after encountering truth . The sixth and final difficulty is becoming a Buddha . He explains that through good deeds as a man, one can approach truth, become a saint, and eventually a Buddha . He notes a growing trend among wealthy individuals globally who seek spiritual enlightenment and meditation, desiring a life similar to Buddha's, rather than dying from illnesses like Steve Jobs . This indicates an increasing value placed on truth .


## 01:00:00 The Value of Truth and the Path to Becoming a Buddha
The speaker plans to establish a "Heo Kyung-young Truth Experience Center" for meditation, where people can simply sit before his picture, call his name, and read his books to experience healing and spiritual transformation . He claims his picture contains special energy that can cure illnesses . He emphasizes that becoming a Buddha means going to a better world . While Earth is a good place, being born in a prosperous country like Korea, with its 40,000 temples, is an immense blessing, given the low probability among the world's population . Meeting him, the speaker, is an even rarer and more fortunate event, akin to directly meeting God . He explains that humans cannot directly meet God but can only encounter representatives . He concludes by stating that humans, through their continuous cycle of rebirth, cannot fully grasp the entirety of truth .
